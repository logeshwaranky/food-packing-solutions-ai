# Data Sources and Provenance Documentation

## 1. Initial Starter Dataset
- **Filename**: `food_packing_ai_dataset.xlsx`
- **Location**: `/data/food_packing_ai_dataset.xlsx`
- **License**: Educational and Research Open License
- **Structure**:
  1. **Food Varieties**: 103 items spanning Prepared Meals, Produce, Beverages, Snacks, Condiments, Staples, Bakery, Dairy, Confectionery, Meat, and Seafood.
  2. **Packaging Materials**: 20 standard material substrates (Glass, PET, HDPE, PP, LDPE, Paperboard, Aluminium foil, Steel/tinplate, Aseptic laminates, Retort laminates, EVOH, Vacuum bags, MAP, Compostable polymers, Moulded pulp, Reusables, Woven sacks, Mesh bags).
  3. **AI Mapping**: 103 expert-style seed mappings connecting food characteristics to packaging candidates with validation priorities.
  4. **AI Tasks**: 8 AI task definitions for category classification, packaging recommendation, and shelf-life prediction.
  5. **Read Me**: Dataset scope, limitations, and mandatory food safety disclosures.

## 2. Official Regulatory Standards Cited
- **US Food and Drug Administration (FDA)**
  - *21 CFR Part 177*: Indirect Food Additives: Polymers (e.g., 177.1520 for Olefin polymers).
  - *21 CFR Part 175*: Adhesives and Components of Coatings (e.g., 175.300 for Resinous and polymeric coatings on metal substrates).
  - *21 CFR Part 176*: Paper and Paperboard Components (e.g., 176.170 for aqueous and fatty foods).
- **European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) / European Commission**
  - *Regulation (EU) No 10/2011*: On plastic materials and articles intended to come into contact with food (Overall Migration Limit of 10 mg/dm² or 60 mg/kg food).
  - *Framework Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004*: Materials and articles intended to come into contact with food.
- **Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)**
  - *Food Safety and Standards (Packaging) Regulations, 2018*: General and specific requirements for food packaging materials.
